Spaghetti and Meatballs

Description

This recipe is for a delicious batch of spaghetti and meatballs that will surely fill up your entire family, plus some! It uses ground beef and Italian sausage create firm but juicy meatballs that will be delectable. You also will make your sauce seemingly from scratch. Get ready for a wonderful meal that is sure to become a household classic!

This recipe was copied from the recipe on the Simply Recipes website. Please refer to that page for any questions regarding the recipe as well as additional suggestions.

Ingredients

    For the sauce

  • 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 sweet yellow onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 1 cup very finely chopped carrots
  • 1 cup chopped cremini brown mushrooms
  • 3 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 2 (28-ounce) cans Italian plum tomatos
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh Italian parsley
  • 1/2 cup chopped fresh basil
  • 1/4 cup red wine
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan-Romano cheese blend
  • Salt, to taste

    • For the meatballs

  • 1 pound ground beef (at least 16% fat)
  • 1/2 pound fresh bulk Italian-style pork sausage
  • 2 tablespoons finely chopped basil
  • 1/2 cup finely chopped cremini brown mushrooms
  • 2 large eggs
  • 3/4 cup unseasoned breadcrumbs
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan-Romano Cheese blend
  • 2 teaspoons sea salt
  • 2 teaspoons fresh ground black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 splash red wine

    • For the pasta

  • 1 1/2 pounds dry 100% semonline spaghetti, thin spaghetti, or bucatini
  • Salt, for the pasta water

Steps

  1. Prepare the sauce

    2. Heat the olive oil in a 5 quart pot on medium to high heat. Add onions and cook for 2 minutes. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds or until fragrant

    Add the carrots and mushrooms, and cook for 2 minutes. Add the tomato paste, stir, and cook for a minute.

    Add the canned tomatoes, parsley, and basil. Mash and stir tomatoes with a potato masher until the sauce starts to thicken, about minutes minutes over medium-high heat.

    Reduce the heat to low and simmer gently while preparing meatballs, stirring occasionally.

  2. Form the meatballs

    3. In a large bowl, use hands to mix the beef, Italian sausage, basil, parsley, mushrooms, eggs, breadcumbs, cheese, salt, and pepper. Do not over-mix or the meatballs will be tough.

    Use a small melon baller, cookie scoop, or heaping teaspoon to form 1-inch round meatballs. Roll and compress into tight balls, but don't overwork the mixture.

  3. Brown the meatballs

    4. In a separate wide, shallow pan, heat the olive oil on medium-high heat. Brown the meatballs on all sides, about 2 to 3 minutes.

    Depending on pan size you may need to brown 2 batches of the meatballs. Cook them in a single layer; do not crowd the pan by stacking them.

    Do not overcook the meatballs; they will continue to cook in the sauce. You just wnat to brown them on all sides. Towards the end of browning the meatballs, add a little bit of red wine to deglaze the pan.

  4. Finish and simmer the sauce

    5. Add the red wine to the sauce and sitr in the cheese. Add salt to taste. Add the browned meatballs and gently stir. Simmer the sauce and meatballs for 30 to 45 mintes, stirring occasionally.

  5. Cook the spaghetti

    6. While the sauce is simmering, bring a large pot with 4 quarts of salted water to a boil (1 tablespoon of salt for every 2 quarts of water). When the water returns to a boil add the pasta to the pot.

    Leave the pot uncovered while you cook the pasta on high heat with a vigorous boil. Put a timer on for 8 to 10 minutes, or according to package direction for al dente pasta. Drain.

  6. Serve

    7. To serve, place a thin layer of sauce on each plate, add the pasta, add sauce and meatballs, and then sprinkle with grated Parmesan-Romano Cheese.
